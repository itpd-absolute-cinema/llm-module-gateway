# Sprint Retrospective — SPRINT-08

## What Went Well

1. The three UT-01 sessions ran on schedule, and the sanitized notes were written on the day of each session.
2. The results found a clear, actionable blocker: the create-plugin entry point.
3. The configuration plan was written from the repository, not from memory, and its five groups are complete.
4. The configuration evaluation named a first change that is small and concrete.

## What Did Not

1. SEQ and SUS were not collected. **Root cause:** the session template did not include the questionnaires in the observation flow, and the facilitator did not pause after each task to ask.
2. The sessions ran short, and Task 3 was not reached in one session. **Root cause:** the Task 1 time limit was too generous and the session had no hard stop.
3. The findings were written the day after the sessions, not on the same day. **Root cause:** the observer's raw notes were not structured for a quick write-up.
