# AI Usage

AI assistance was used during Assignment 4 to help clarify requirements, organize project documentation, and review existing materials.

## Areas of AI Assistance

ChatGPT and an OpenAI coding assistant were used for:

- Understanding assignment requirements and organizing the work.
- Drafting documentation and improving its wording and structure.
- Reviewing technical approaches and comparing them with existing project materials.
- Preparing follow-up questions and organizing information from earlier reports.
- Checking formatting, links, and documentation consistency.

AI output was used as a starting point for drafts and revisions.
Technical statements were checked against project documents and source code.

## Human Review and Responsibility

The team reviews the drafts and decides what goes into the submission.
Customer answers and decisions must come from meeting records, not generated text.
