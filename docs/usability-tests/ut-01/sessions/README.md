# Session Template

## Welcome

Thank the participant. Explain that the session tests the product, not the participant.

## Consent and recording check

Read the consent. Confirm the screen recording is on and no audio is captured. If the participant withdraws, stop the session.

## Pre-test

Ask the pre-test questions in [`../surveys.md`](../surveys.md).

## Observation (no think-aloud)

Ask the participant to work on the task silently. The observer records what the participant does: where they click, where they hesitate, what they try first.

## Scenario reading

Read Task 1's scenario. Do not name a button, label, screen, step, or step count.

## No-help rule

Say: "I cannot help while you work. If something does not work, that is the product's problem, not yours."

## Observation sheet

The observer records: what the participant tried first, where they hesitated, any assistance given and when, the time, and the outcome.

## SEQ after each task

Ask the SEQ question after each task. Record the answer.

## SUS and open-ended post-test after all tasks

Ask the SUS items, then the open-ended post-test.

## Debrief

Thank the participant. Confirm the screen recording and the data handling.

## Stop rule

Stop a task at its time limit or when the participant gives up. Record `Failed`.
