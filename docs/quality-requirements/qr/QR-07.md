# QR-07: Usable gateway setup

New users rate setting up the gateway and obtaining their first response at a median of at least 5 out of 7 for ease.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Operability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

Operability fits the perceived effort of completing setup; the main task's Single Ease Question (SEQ) measures that effort directly from the participant's perspective.
The team selected the 5/7 bar before sessions: the middle participant should find setup on the easy side of the scale, rather than neutral or difficult.
Technical risk is medium because the path exists but setup documentation and deployment configuration currently disagree.
Completion, assistance, time, and overall usability remain separate reported results, not additional pass criteria.

## Scenario

### Source

A developer who has used Docker but is new to this gateway, matching UT-01's participant profile.

### Stimulus

The participant attempts to set up the gateway and obtain a first response using its documentation.
Web search and AI assistance are also available under UT-01's fixed tool rules.

### Environment

Staging on the university network, with Docker and Compose already installed, a fresh checkout of the same release, and the gateway stopped and unconfigured.
Reset synthetic seed data before each session and use the stub provider.
UT-01 defines the preparation and the selected setup limit of 20 minutes.
The ease rating describes this workflow with the allowed tools, not documentation alone.

### Artifact

The gateway setup workflow and its documentation.

### Response

The participant attempts setup and rates its ease after the attempt, including when it fails.

### Response Measure

Across at least three completed, eligible UT-01 sessions, the median main-task SEQ is at least 5 on the 1–7 scale.

## QRT-01

Read the main-task ease ratings from UT-01 and compare their median with the requirement.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md), against staging on the university network.
- **Why Manual:** Only a participant can report perceived ease after attempting setup; an automated request cannot judge that perception. This test reads no SLI.

### Procedure

1. Confirm eligibility against UT-01's participant profile and finish its three tasks, protocol, consent, and instrumentation checks before the first counted session; freeze the measure and bar before collecting data.
2. Run the agreed main task with at least three eligible participants under UT-01's protocol, aiming for five completed sessions. Collect the SEQ after each attempt, whether successful or failed.
3. Complete the remaining session measures and publish sanitized session notes and [results](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md), keeping raw evidence private.
4. Read every counted session's main-task SEQ and calculate the median as the analysis plan specifies. Do not substitute overall SUS scores or omit failed attempts.
5. In SPRINT-08, record `Passes` or `Fails` in the testing plan with the results link, reviewer, and observed median. With fewer than three completed eligible sessions, leave the test `Not verified`.

**Pass when:** At least three completed, eligible UT-01 sessions yield a main-task median SEQ of at least 5/7.
